# Notice: Solid avocado

An avocado of three solid meshes, skin, flesh and pit, rebuilt from the peelable avocado
of `private_meshes` (original model by Yannick Schröder, generated procedurally by
`scripts/generate_avocado.py` there, seed 0). No external meshes, scans, textures or
product references were used.
